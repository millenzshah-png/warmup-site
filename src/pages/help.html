Help

Short answers about the heat, the cells, washing and ordering.

Heat Cells and charging Washing Ordering

Heat

How warm does it get?

You set a skin temperature from {{targetMin}} to {{targetMax}}°F and the hoodie holds it. A panel that reaches {{panelLimit}}°F switches off at once and heats again once it has cooled below {{panelResume}}°F.

Where does it heat?

Left chest, right chest and upper back. Each panel has its own switch in the app.

Can I use it without my phone?

Yes. The button on the hem switches the heat on and off. You need the app to change the temperature, the mode or the schedule.

What do the modes do?

Each mode holds a different temperature: Auto {{modes.1.f}}°F, Sick {{modes.2.f}}°F, Commute {{modes.3.f}}°F, Outdoor {{modes.4.f}}°F and Sleep {{modes.0.f}}°F. See what each mode does.

Cells and charging

How long does the heat last?

From two full cells with all three panels on: {{runtime.0.time}} on Low, {{runtime.1.time}} on Medium and {{runtime.2.time}} on High. A spare pair gives you a second set to swap in.

How do I charge the cells?

Snap them out and set them on the dock. It charges both cells at once over USB-C at {{dockW}} W. Empty to full takes {{chargeTime}}.

Can I keep wearing it while a cell charges?

Yes. The hoodie runs on one cell while the other is on the dock.

How long do the cells last before they wear out?

Each cell is rated for {{cellCycles}} charge cycles.

Washing

Can I machine wash it?

Yes, with the cells out. Snap out both cells, close the side seam flaps, turn the hoodie inside out, then wash cold on a gentle cycle. It is rated for {{washes}} washes. See the four washing steps.

Can it go in the dryer, and can I iron it?

No dryer. Hang it to dry, and never iron on the panels.

What is wash mode?

A lock in the app. While it is on, the hoodie will not heat, even if the button on the hem is pressed.
