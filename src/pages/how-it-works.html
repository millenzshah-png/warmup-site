How the hoodie holds your temperature

You pick a number from {{targetMin}} to {{targetMax}}°F. The hoodie reads your skin and does the rest.

  1. The collar sensor reads your skin

    It takes a reading every few seconds.

  2. The hem controller checks it against your setting

    Below your number, the panels switch on. Once your skin is back at your number, they rest.

  3. Three panels pulse heat

    They pulse instead of running flat out, which stretches the charge.

    • Right chest
    • Upper back

Thin graphene panels on your chest and upper back

Two on the chest and one across the upper back. Each has its own switch in the app, so you can heat your back and leave your chest off.

The controller watches every panel

Each panel has its own temperature reading. The controller in the hem acts on it by itself, with or without your phone.

{{panelLimit}}°F
A panel that reaches this switches off at once.
{{panelResume}}°F
It heats again once it has cooled below this.
{{tripsBeforeLock}} times
A panel that cuts off this often within {{tripWindowMin}} minutes is switched off for the rest of the day, unless you switch it back on in the app.

Two cells sit in the side seams

Each cell weighs {{cellG}} g and snaps onto a magnetic connector at the waist. With one on each side, neither side pulls down and nothing hangs on your back.

Swap a cell in seconds

Pull a cell straight off its connector. There is no cable to unplug.

Wear it on one cell while the other charges

Charge one on the dock and keep the hoodie on. The app shows the charge of each cell.
