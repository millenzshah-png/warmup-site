The Warmup app

Set the temperature once and the hoodie holds it. The app is free and works on iPhone and Android.

Open the app (opens in a new tab)

This browser version runs with a simulated hoodie, so you can try every screen before you own one.

Five modes built in

93°FAuto
Reads the collar sensor every few seconds and pulses heat to keep you level.
95°FSick
Gentle, steady warmth for chills. It ramps up slowly and switches off after 2 hours. Comfort, not treatment: see a doctor if a fever lasts more than two days.
96°FCommute
Warm by the time you step out. Once you have been still indoors for five minutes it drops to Low, and it switches off after 90 minutes.
97°FOutdoor
Uses the outside temperature from your phone. The colder it is out there, the closer it holds to your target.
91°FSleep
Low, even heat while you settle. It fades out on its own and switches off.

You can also save modes of your own, at any temperature from 88 to 99°F.

Schedule, zones and battery

Preheat before you leave

Pick your days and a leave time, then choose how far ahead it starts: 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes. It needs a charged cell in the hoodie and wash mode off.

A switch for each panel

Left chest, right chest and upper back each have a switch, with the panel's temperature next to it.

The charge of each cell

See the left and right cell and the time left at your level. The app warns you when the pair drops below 20%.

Wash mode locks the heat

Turn it on before washing so the hoodie cannot heat by accident. It stays locked even if the button on the hem is pressed.

Updates
