How the heating works

Machine washable with the cells out

  1. Snap out both cells

    They pull straight off the magnetic connector.

  2. Close the side seam flaps

    That keeps the connectors from catching.

  3. Turn the hoodie inside out

    That protects the hem controller and the panels.

  4. Wash cold on a gentle cycle, then hang dry

    Never iron on the panels. The hoodie is rated for 50 washes.

Turn on wash mode in the app first. It locks the heating so the hoodie cannot heat by accident.
